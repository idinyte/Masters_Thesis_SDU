\section{Conclusion}
Two exoskeletons that allow the user to feel the touch of virtual objects in PyBullet simulation have been created. The second exoskeleton is an improvement over the first one, as the global position tracking allows for movement in a virtual environment. The linear motion of the second exoskeleton captures the movement of the robot gripper more accurately than the circular movement trajectories of the first exoskeleton servo arms. Also, the second exoskeleton has only one motor, resulting in cost and weight savings. The price of the exoskeleton is competitive as a lot of parts are 3D printed, but the torque-to-force transmission has only 56.93\% efficiency, leaving room for improvement. The force controller can successfully smooth the force signal coming from the PyBullet simulation using exponential smoothing, providing a more pleasant exoskeleton experience for the user.
